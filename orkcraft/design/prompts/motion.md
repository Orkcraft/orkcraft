One layer of a building's house for Orkcraft, a town of AI agents drawn as an orc war camp, that moves while its ork
works (docs/design/building-motion.md). The house is painted as two layers: the house without its moving part, and the
moving part alone. The town lays them over each other and turns or swings the part in code, so each layer must lie on
the reference pixel for pixel.

Paint it in the style of the reference picture exactly: the same painted pixel-art look, the same palette (moss and
dark greens, ivory for tusks, horns and bones, gold accents, near-black for doors and openings), the same light from
the top left, the same level of detail and the same chunky pixels.

The same canvas size and aspect ratio as the reference, and what you keep at EXACTLY the same scale and in EXACTLY the
same position as in the reference: never move, rescale, re-centre or redraw it. Transparent background (a PNG with
alpha), no ground, no shadow, no scenery, no text, no UI.
