The head of the ork who lives in a building of Orkcraft, under its building's hat (docs/design/sprites.md).

Pixel art in the style of Warcraft II (1995), and of the reference picture exactly: the head of a tired orkish office
worker in a flat 2D front view, an almost perfect circle with no neck and no body, muted moss-green skin in three
shades, a 1 px dark green outline, heavy straight brows, half-lidded pale-yellow eyes, two pale bone tusks running up
the lower cheeks, a flat mouth; the hat on top says the building. Palette only #12261b #2f5e46 #4f8a45 #6aa84f
#93c45a #e6e2b8 #6e5470 #d8c79a #a89470 #1a1a1a. Chunky pixels, very low detail, a calm, deadpan expression, no
shadow, no ground, no text.

Transparent background (a PNG with alpha). The same canvas size and aspect ratio as the reference.
