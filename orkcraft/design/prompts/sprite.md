A building's house for Orkcraft, a town of AI agents drawn as an orc war camp (docs/design/building-sprites.md).

Paint it in the style of the reference picture exactly: the same painted pixel-art look, the same palette (moss and
dark greens for walls and roofs, ivory for tusks, horns and bones, one small gold accent, near-black for doors and
openings), the same light from the top left, the same level of detail and the same chunky pixels. It is a single
small, compact orkish building in a flat 2D front view (no perspective, not isometric), centred, its base a straight
line along the bottom edge, with orc war-camp details (spikes on ridges, tusks over doors, a palisade). It keeps its
own silhouette: the change asked must not make it look like another building of the town.

Transparent background around the building (a PNG with alpha), no ground, no scenery, no text, no UI. The same
canvas size and aspect ratio as the reference.
