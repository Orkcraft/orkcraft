# Design — the settlement: the orks' joy, leisure on the grass, the home

Status: design, 15 October 2026, from the owner's note "Орочий посёлок: карта, дом и довольство" and his answers to
the PM's questions the same day. Not built. Builds on decisions-2026-10.md §20 (the lines), §24 (feedback), growth.md,
v3-prompts.md §14 (items).

**The principle (the owner):** productivity first, the game second. A person comes to get work done; the game gives
a second of joy for it and never asks for attention, never blocks the work, never needs looking after.

## 1. The orks' joy

- **What makes the orks glad: the person's decisions,** not the number of tasks: passing *or* sending back a cart at
  the Review gate, answering an ork's question, fixing a building (a 🔥 cleared, an improvement taken). A send-back is
  a decision too and gladdens them as much as a pass (§24: critique is an order on the work, never a blow).
  Counting tasks would reward spending more of the person's window; decisions reward being in the loop.
- **Glad, then neutral; never sad.** After a decision the orks are visibly glad for a while (a cheer, the train's
  whistle, a dance by the bar); otherwise they are neutral. **There is no decay and no scale that falls**, so a week
  away costs nothing (calm by default).
- **Where it shows:** on the orks themselves on the map (a short cheer at the moment, then calm), and **on the orks'
  icons at the bottom** (the faces in the 🥩 list and the panels wear a small glad mark while glad). No number, no bar
  in the HUD.
- **The score stays the game's measure** (the-score.md: wood and window per accepted result, quality guarded); joy is
  the feeling, the score is the honest number.

## 2. Leisure on the grass: the person's places, the orks' rest

- **The grass is the person's:** they place, freely as items are placed today (snap to the grass grid, never on
  track, beds, paths or against a work building), **relaxing things**: bushes, benches, flower beds, fountains, a bar,
  a carousel, an arena, the class's own piece (a mausoleum for the managers' class, "buried at work"; a pig farm for
  the orks' class), the seasonal decor.
- **Orks rest among them:** an ork with no work walks the camp's paths and the grass to a bench, the bar, the
  fountain, the carousel, and back to its building when work comes. The camp looks alive by itself.
- **They do nothing to the work:** leisure pieces never speed or slow anything; they are where the orks enjoy the
  joy the person's decisions gave them.
- **Where they come from:** some from the start, others earned (deeds, the campaign, lean weeks: v3-prompts §14's
  rule that rewards are placed things, never powers); coins to buy them are a second stage (§5).

## 3. Work buildings have no levels

Work buildings no longer show levels (the Renown footings and banners of growth.md §5 / design-system-v3 §4.12 are
retired from the map). A building's speed or quality, when shown, shows **in its look**: more smoke from the chimney,
lights in the windows, a few details, never a separate level or an annex.

## 4. The home: the person's own room

- **A screen of its own**, entered from the home on line 0.5 (its menu, decisions §20, gains *Enter the home*), never
  in the way of the work.
- **The person's figure stands inside** and grows from their decisions (five or six stages of look; growth.md's
  stages, now driven by the decisions of §1 as well as the missions and lean weeks). If it grew since the last visit,
  it changes clothes on the way in, once.
- **Deeds hang on the walls as trophies** by themselves; nothing to arrange. They leave the portrait's menu. Deeds not
  yet earned show as **plain silhouettes** (no question mark: "?" is the yellow ❓ signal's).
- **Later, if the joy works:** coins, furniture, floors and walls, free arrangement.

## 5. Coins (second stage)

Earned by the person's decisions (§1) without any clicking to collect; spent on the home's furniture and on leisure
pieces for the grass. Not before the first stage has shown the joy works.

## 6. Unchanged

- **Names stay** (CLAUDE.md): the train theme is the look (the dispatcher's booth, the depot, the weighbridge), never
  new words for buildings.
- **Hiding the game** is the Office look, as today.

## 7. MVP (first stage)

1. Joy from decisions: the cheer on the map, the glad mark on the orks' icons, neutral otherwise.
2. Idle orks walking to leisure pieces.
3. Leisure pieces as items (a first set of 6–8, art via the owner's Gemini flow) placed freely on the grass.
4. No levels on work buildings; quality by look later.
5. The home's room: the figure and the deeds on the walls, silhouettes for the rest.
Coins, furniture and quality-by-look wait.
