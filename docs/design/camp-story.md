# Design — the camp's story: work comes in, goods fly out

Status: proposal 2026-10-13, the owner's ask: almost every cart enters through External listeners, the Task board or
the town's clock, and sooner or later leaves through the Publisher, drawn in Camp as a catapult. Can the story and the
name play on it? Builds on [color-pass.md](color-pass.md) §6d (rails), [the-score.md](the-score.md),
[naming.md](naming.md), [audience.md](audience.md).

## 1. The story in one line

> **Raw work comes in, the orks refine it on the line, and finished goods are catapulted over the wall into the
> world.**

It is how the product already works, and it is a factory game's own shape (raw material in, products out), told
in the camp's voice.

| the product | the camp's story | drawn as |
|---|---|---|
| External listeners, the Task board, the clock | **the ways in:** a cart rolls in through a gap in the palisade (a message spotted from the lookout, a task pinned on the board, a cart the clock sends at its hour) | the track **starts outside the wall** at each way in |
| the Agent pool and the other buildings | **the line:** the orks refine the cart, building by building | the track between buildings |
| the Review gate | **the check:** nothing leaves without the Warchief's nod (the person) | the gate on the track |
| the Publisher | **the way out:** the finished cart is flung over the wall: a pull request opened, a post published, a reply sent | **a catapult at the palisade, the track's end** |

**The words stay** (CLAUDE.md): the interface says External listeners, Task board, Publisher; the gap in the wall and
the catapult are only how Camp draws them. The camp's voice (the Warchief's lines, growth news, posts) may say
"launched".

## 2. What it gives

1. **A climax for every cart, and for the GIF.** The launch is the moment of success: the catapult swings, the
   scroll flies over the wall, "PR #42 opened". The GIF ends there, not at the gate: a developer's "ship it", drawn.
2. **The score reads as a factory's:** **goods launched**, and the wood and limit each one cost
   ([the-score.md](the-score.md)). "12 launched this week, 30 % less wood each" is a line people share.
3. **Every cart's life has a shape the person learns once:** in at a gap, along the line, through the gate, over the
   wall. A cart that never launches (stuck, dropped) is the exception the town shows.
4. **The phone's "since your last look"** starts with what was launched ([audience.md](audience.md) §2a): the
   reward first.
5. **Deeds** ([growth.md](growth.md)): the first launch, a hundred launched, a week with no failed launch.

## 3. Held to

- **Not everything leaves:** a cart may end in the Inspector, a wiki page or the person's hands; the catapult is the
  usual end, not the only one.
- **The launch is never automatic for what cannot be taken back:** what leaves the town passes the gate
  ([audience.md](audience.md) §4a). The catapult fires only after the gate.
- **One launch, one small celebration,** short and quiet: a flight and a line, never a sound or a confetti storm on
  every cart, and never during working hours' quiet ([work-and-play.md](work-and-play.md)).

## 4. The name, read with the story

The story is a line from the ways in to the catapult, so a name for **the line** fits it best:

| name | with the story |
|---|---|
| **OrkMine** | a mine is where the ore comes *from*, inside the camp; here the work comes from outside and leaves as goods: the picture fits (rails, carts), the story less; and "mine" may read as crypto ([naming.md](naming.md) §6) |
| **Tuskline** ([naming.md](naming.md) §2) | a line: the rail line and the production line; no "ork", no "-craft" |
| **Orkline** | the same, keeping "ork" (and its question) |
| **Catapult-** names | the climax, but a catapult is one building; the line is the product |

**Product view:** the story adds weight to a "-line" name. It does not decide alone: the lawyer's hour and the five
developers decide (Q18).
