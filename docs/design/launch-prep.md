## 2. The marketing's preparation, started this week

Materials, the landing page and GitHub, so that from 19 October posts have somewhere to point.

### 2a. GitHub: found, and understood in five seconds

- **The description** (the one line search shows): "A town for your coding agents: tasks go down roads, through a
  review gate, and the game is spending less per accepted result. Claude Code, Codex. Local-first."
- **Topics** (GitHub's search and Google read them): `claude-code`, `codex`, `coding-agents`, `ai-agents`,
  `multi-agent`, `agent-orchestration`, `developer-tools`, `local-first`, `pixel-art`, `gamification`,
  `side-project`.
- **The social preview image** (shown on every shared link): the Camp frame with one ❓ and a cart at the gate, the
  name and the line; 1280×640.
- **The README's first screen** says what the others do not ([similar-products.md](similar-products.md) §4):
  - the headline today, "Run many coding agents in one project — and see them like a real-time strategy game",
    **is the category's** (Pixel Agents says the same): replaced by the story of [audience.md](audience.md) §3, "build
    the factory that builds your side project";
  - the GIF: a cart from the board to the gate with its pull request (the hero GIF's placeholder is still there);
  - Office and Camp side by side ([work-and-play.md](work-and-play.md) §4b); "checked on macOS" said plainly
    (Q15); no phone promise (cut-list §3);
  - a short "How is this different from …" section, with respect.
- **Releases with notes** (GitHub lists active repositories higher, and people read them as signs of life); a
  `good first issue` label on a few real tasks, for the community.

### 2b. The landing page (orkcraft.dev)

One page, the same order as the README, so neither is written twice:

1. the line and the GIF; install in one command;
2. the commute scene ([audience.md](audience.md) §2a), in three pictures: a day of others' work → the phone on the
   way home, what was built → the laptop at home, the camp;
3. what makes it a factory: carts, roads, the gate, the score;
4. Office at work, Camp after work;
5. how it differs; what it is not (no cloud, no API keys, your own subscriptions);
6. the community link (where people gather) and GitHub.

**For search:** a title and description naming the words people search ("Claude Code", "multiple coding agents",
"agent orchestration"); one page fast to load; the videos as files with a poster image. The existing landing video
(`docs/landing/videos/`) shows mail and Slack triage: the "and more", not the first story. **A new one is filmed for
the developer story**, once the GIF's flow works.
