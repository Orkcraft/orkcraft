# Orkline — the testers' guide (one page)

**Thank you for testing.** Orkline is a camp where work rides from the board through the orks to the Review gate, and nothing passes without you. About 10 minutes for the first session; then use it as you
like for a week.

**What stays yours.** Everything runs on your machine: no account, no API keys (it uses your own Claude Code or Codex
login), nothing sent anywhere by itself. Anonymous usage stats are off until you say yes, and never include code,
prompts, paths or names. The first real work (quest 4) only reads your repository.

## Before you start
- macOS (Linux should work, tell me if not). Claude Code or Codex installed and signed in. A repository of your own.
- Install: `[install line from the README]`, then `orkline gui`.

## The first session
1. The first screen asks what kind of builder you are and confirms the AI tools it found.
2. **Mission 1** walks you through raising your camp: the Task board, the Agent pool, the Review gate. Quests 1–3 cost
   nothing and call no model.
3. Before quest 4 the camp asks to connect its hooks to your AI tool. It shows exactly what it writes and how to take
   it back. Quest 4 does real, read-only work on your repository; its price is said first.

## During the week
- Try "I'm off" in the morning and "since your last look" when you come back.
- Try Office at work, Camp in the evening.
- Anything confusing or broken: the Discord's #testers channel, or reply to my message. A screenshot helps.

## The questions (I'll ask after the first session, and after a week)

**After the first session**
1. What do you think Orkline is for, in one sentence?
2. Where did you get stuck or confused? (the quest, the screen)
3. Was the first win (an ork's question answered) clear? How long did it take?
4. Did you finish quest 4? If not, why?
5. At the Review gate: how long did it take you to decide to pass or send back a result? Was it clear what was
   done and what it cost?
6. How do you check your agents' work today: each result (diff, PR), the plan before they start, or in a batch once a
   day? Which of these would you want at the Review gate?
7. Did it feel like a game or like a tool? (1 = a tool, 5 = a game) What made it feel so?
8. Do you need icons, illustrations or sprites for what you build, and where do you get them today?

**After a week**
1. How many times did you open it? On which device, and when (evenings, between work tasks, on the way home)?
2. Do you work from home, at an office, or both? Did you leave the laptop running with "I'm off"?
3. When you opened it on a short break, did it feel like a break or like work?
4. What did the orks build for you this week? Was it worth what it cost? Did you come back to the Review gate on your
   own, and why?
5. Would you be disappointed if you couldn't use it any more? (very / somewhat / not)
6. What one thing would make you use it every day?
7. If you could keep only one building, which one, and why?
