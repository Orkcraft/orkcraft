# Camp's v3 sprites

Cut by `python tools/camp_sheet.py --v3` from the owner's generated sheets in `docs/design/img/references/v3/`
(the sheets' names and sizes: `tools/camp_sheets_v3.json`, [docs/design/design-system-v3.md](../../../docs/design/design-system-v3.md) §8).
Drawn at half and shown at ×2; every size a multiple of 8; colours snapped to the v3 tokens. `manifest.json` lists
every sprite cut, with its size, frames, anchor and source sheet. Empty until the first sheet arrives; v2's
placeholders (`../camp/`) stay until I1 switches the town to these. Generated art, kept until the product proves
itself (docs/design/decisions-2026-10.md §13.4).
