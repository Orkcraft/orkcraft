# Sprites in this folder

Camp's finished pixel-art sprites, named as the Sprites section lays out. Every sprite is shown with `image-rendering: pixelated` at 1× or a whole multiple.

- `buildings/catapult/header.png` (76×40), `buildings/loot/header.png` (80×33), `buildings/workshop/header.png` (80×40), `buildings/scrolls/header.png` (75×40) and `buildings/custom/header.png` (80×35), each with `@2x`: the S headers. The plank under Custom and Loot Vault and the black line under The Catapult were cut off; Workshop keeps its stone footing and Scroll Dump its floor, which are narrower than the card.
- `buildings/signpost/header.png` (44×40), `buildings/pit/header.png` (64×28) and `buildings/horn/header.png` (50×32), each with `@2x`: the last headers. The plank or earth band under each was cut off.
- `buildings/mill/header.png` (60×32) and `@2x`: the Mill as a water mill, a plank hut with a big wooden waterwheel at its right side, a chute pouring a thin blue stream onto it and a gold axle hub. It replaces the windmill. The thin ground line under it was cut off. The Mill keeps the wooden frame the drawing put around it, and The Pit's board has a wider gold frame than the brief's one tiny accent. **All 19 catalog buildings now have a header.**
- `doodads/forest/tree-1.png` (14×21) and `@2x`: a dark pine, reduced from a 32×48 drawing. In each 2×2 block, an edge block keeps its outline colour and an inner block keeps its most common colour.
- `doodads/ice/mountain-1.png` (27×17) and `@2x`: an icy peak with a snow cap, reduced the same way. The full-width ground band of the original was cut off.
- `roads/straight.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: the first road tile, an 8px track of packed planks and earth through the middle of the tile, reduced from a 2000px drawing. It is used in the Road preview.
- `roads/end.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: the dead end, a road coming in from the left and ending in a rounded cap. Its left half is the straight tile's own pixels, so the two join without a seam; the cap was redrawn in the straight tile's palette, since the generated tile came in a flatter brown with different ruts.
- `roads/gate-out.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: the exit gate, where a road leaves its building: the dead end mirrored, so the rounded cap is on the left, with a gold arrow on a short post pointing along the road. The generated tile was reduced to a 3px gold smudge, so the arrow was redrawn by hand at 8×8 in the house gold and outline.
- `carts/minecart.png` (20×18) and `@2x`: the iron mine cart with a riveted gold rim and a heap of amber ore, in side profile. It is symmetric, so it serves both directions. The thin ground line under the drawing was cut off. It replaces the wooden handcarts (`carts/cart-right.png` and `cart-left.png`), which blended into the road.
- `carts/cart-down.png` (20×18) and `@2x`: the same mine cart from the front, coming towards you, for carts on vertical roads. The wheels show as two short stubs under the tub. Until a back view exists, it also serves carts moving up.
- `orks/ork.png` (24×16) and `@2x`: the ork, the ork mark's flat head (`../logo/`, drawn by `tools/logo.py`): green, pointed ears, a dark brow band and two pale tusks, on its 12×8 grid at 2×. It replaces the round shaded head and 🧌 in badges, the roster and the War Map.
- `orks/ork-portrait.png` (46×38) and `@2x`: the same head at 3×, standing at the bottom of the portrait slot, for the asking ork in Dialog.
- `buildings/town_hall/header.png` (112×56), `buildings/war_drum/header.png` (75×56), `buildings/forest/header.png` (101×56), `buildings/lake/header.png` (109×56) and `buildings/crag/header.png` (96×40), each with `@2x`: the first building headers, reduced to their slots (L 112×56, M 96×48). The full-width ground band under Lake and the plank under Tally Crag were cut off. They are used in the Town preview.
- `buildings/forge/header.png` (75×48), `buildings/council/header.png` (85×48), `buildings/barracks/header.png` (96×46) and `buildings/fields/header.png` (96×44), each with `@2x`: the M headers. The plank under Forge and Task Fields was cut off. Clan Fire is a ring of stones, open in the middle so the ground shows through. The first test Forge (137×114) is replaced.
- `buildings/watchtower/header.png` (37×58) and `@2x`: the orkish lookout tower, after the Warcraft III Horde watch tower: four splayed log legs, a log tower, a platform ringed with sharpened stakes, a pointed hide roof with bone tusks, a gold spike tip and a red war banner. It is narrow by nature, so it is the one header allowed to rise above its slot: 58px high instead of 48, so it reads as clearly as its neighbours. It replaces the squat beacon tower.

Component previews embed these sprites as `data:` URIs, because an upload's `/_blob/` URL does not load inside a preview frame. They embed them only in the Camp half, since Office shows no sprites. The app itself loads the files.
- `fx/fire.png` (64×16) and `@2x`: the flame over a burning building, 4 frames of 16×16 in a row (upright, leaning right, leaning left, tall). Each frame was snapped to the six fire colours by majority vote, so the outline and the yellow core stay crisp at 16px.
- `icons/res-quota.png`, `res-gold.png`, `res-lumber.png`, `res-meat.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: the HUD resources, an hourglass, a stack of coins, two crossed logs and meat on the bone. They replace ⏳ 🪙 🪵 🥩. The logs were redrawn at 16px with a clean outline, because a plain reduction turned their bark lines into noise.
- `icons/chain.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: a wooden signpost with a gold stud, the chain-or-script marker in badges. It replaces 🪧.
- `icons/tier-elder.png`, `tier-warrior.png`, `tier-laborer.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: the tier marks in badges, a violet orb on a wooden stand, two crossed iron swords with gold guards, and an iron pickaxe with a gold collar. They replace 🔮 ⚔ ⛏. The orb's violet is close to the magenta background, so it was keyed on pure magenta only.
- `orks/ork-idle.png`, `ork-busy.png`, `ork-waiting.png` (24×16) and `@2x`: the ork's states, a few pixels changed inside the head by `tools/logo.py`: eyes shut and a sleep mark over the right ear; a pale-blue drop of sweat past it; a flame, orange with a gold core, on the crown.
- `portraits/builder.png` (46×38) and `@2x`: the Builder, an old ork in a dented iron helmet with a candle stub, round spectacles, a grey stubble beard, one tusk and a rolled blueprint behind his ear. Used in Dialog.
- `icons/cmd-preset.png` (32×32) and `@2x`: the Preset command, a gold scroll with a seal. The generated icon came on its own stone button, so the scroll was cut out of it; the `ok-act` button draws the bevel.
- `doodads/forest/stump-1.png` (14×10), `doodads/ice/tree-1.png` (11×21), `doodads/ice/mountain-2.png` (27×11) and `doodads/void/rock-1.png` (20×20), each with `@2x`: decorations, a moss-dark stump, a bare frozen tree with snow on its branches, a low twin peak, and a heap of ash rocks round a broken spire. They are kept at the same scale as the pine and the first peak.
- `carts/cart-up.png` (20×18) and `@2x`: the mine cart from behind, going up a vertical road: a plain iron back panel with a gold coupling ring. The ground line under it was cut off.
- `doodads/forest/tree-2.png` (14×21) and `@2x`: a bare dead tree in the forest's dark moss greens, the second forest tree beside the pine. Its keyed-out edge kept a magenta cast, which was repainted in the outline green.
- `icons/cmd-new.png` and `icons/cmd-audit.png` (32×32) and `@2x`: the New command (a gold hammer crossed with a saw) and the Audit command (a magnifying glass over a scroll). They replace 🛠 and 🔍 on building actions.
- `roads/cross.png` and `roads/gate-in.png` (16×16) and `@2x`: the crossing and the entry gate. Like the dead end, both were built from the straight tile's own pixels so every joint is seamless: the crossing lays the rotated straight over the horizontal one, and the entry gate is the dead end with a gold ring post drawn by hand. The generated tiles came in different colours and widths.
- `orks/ork-advisor.png` (26×28) and `@2x`: the same ork in an office black tie, at the source's own pixel grid. It was the Office advisor at the town's bottom-right corner until the ork mark (`../logo/ork-mark.svg`) took its place.
- `buildings/town_hall`, `war_drum`, `watchtower`, `forge`, `scrolls`, `barracks`, `lake` and `loot` `header.png` and `@2x`: the first sheet of the flat set (docs/design/building-sprites.md), redrawn and cut by `tools/sheet.py --cell 11 --scale 2` onto the ork mark's five colours: a hall with ivory horns and a gold banner, a drum under a roof on posts, a watchtower on bone legs with tusk spikes, a forge with a chimney and a gold furnace mouth, a library hut with an open book and a scroll, a long barracks with three doors, a hut on stilts with a round eye window over the water and a vault with a gold coin over its door. They replace the Warcraft 2 headers of these eight.
- `buildings/mill`, `horn`, `signpost`, `pit`, `catapult`, `workshop`, `fields` and `council` `header.png` and `@2x`: the second sheet of the flat set, redrawn bolder and cut like the third (`tools/sheet.py --cell 16.6 --scale 2`, the sheet's labels painted over first): a hut with a water wheel and a gold hub (52×44), a great ivory horn with a gold rim on a stand (50×46), a signpost with two ivory boards (46×48), a ring of stones round a pit under a gold arrow (42×48), a catapult with an ivory arm and a gold stone (50×36), a workshop with a gold pickaxe over its door (50×46), a fenced field with a small signpost (50×52) and a ring of standing stones round a gold fire (46×46). They replace the finer first drawing of this sheet.

- `buildings/forest`, `crag` and `custom` `header.png` and `@2x`: the third sheet of the flat set (`--cell 16.6 --scale 2`): three pines behind a bone fence with a gold gate, a rock with ivory notches and a gold flag, and a hut with ivory horns and a gold question-mark sign. With them every catalog building has its flat header.
