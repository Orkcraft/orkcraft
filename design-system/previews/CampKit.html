Camp kit

The design system v2's components for the new Camp (docs/design/design-system-v2.md, components.md · Camp kit), drawn here from the 0.3 reference sheets.

Reference art. These sprites are the owner's generated 0.3 sheets, cut by the v2 pipeline (magenta and its purple fringe keyed, snapped to each sheet's ~6 px grid, trimmed). They show the system's slots, sizes and behaviour. They are not the art that ships: the commissioned artist draws that to docs/design/artist-brief.md. Where a piece breaks a v2 rule (an isometric tower, a facade on its own island, roof pieces framed on all sides), the preview shows it as it is and the caption says so.
